HERMES / FIDUNIO 0.8.1.5 — FINAL IPAD TOOLBAR LAYOUT

PURPOSE
UI-only refinement of the working 0.8.1.4 iPad two-pane layout.

WHAT CHANGED
- On iPad/tablet wide layout, all 8 attachment tools now appear on ONE horizontal line:
  Photo | File | Voice | Location | Contact | Checklist | Schedule | Saved
- Colored 2D icons remain.

  Messages | Groups | Contacts | Settings
