HERMES / FIDUNIO 0.8.1.6 — TABLET WIDTH + TOOLBAR FIX

PURPOSE
UI-only correction to the 0.8.1.5 iPad layout.

ROOT CAUSE FIXED
The phone composer was still position:fixed and used the 520px phone-width centering calculation even while the app was in tablet mode. That caused the composer/tool row to float over the split view, compress the visible area, and make the 8 icons look odd.

CHANGES
- Tablet composer is now contained inside the RIGHT chat pane.
- Composer width follows the right pane instead of the old phone 520px width.
- All 8 attachment icons remain on ONE line on tablet.
- Icons are compact, colored, and evenly spaced.
- Two-pane breakpoint lowered from 820px to 700px so iPad portrait/narrow Safari widths stay in tablet mode.
- iPhone remains single-pane below 700px.
- Sidebar bottom navigation no longer sits underneath the floating composer.

NO SECURITY/BACKEND CHANGES
No Firebase, Firestore Rules, E2EE, contact verification, device identity, Outbox, local history, or reconnect changes.
